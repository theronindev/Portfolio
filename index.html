About Me

I'm Samer Jameel, a software engineer based in Baghdad, Iraq. I spent my first seven years in network engineering and IT operations before pivoting deliberately into software — and never looked back. Since then I've shipped a dozen production applications, ten of them as the sole engineer at Al-Mouyasser, with TanCafe live on both the App Store and Google Play. I build cross-platform mobile apps in Flutter and React Native, backed by REST APIs I write in PHP, Node.js, and Go over SQL Server, Oracle, and Firebase. My favourite problems are the unglamorous ones: offline-first sync, industrial barcode hardware, and replacing hours of manual work with something that just runs.
